# Data Source Disclaimers

GridPulse NH is an educational / portfolio project. It is **not** official software of, or affiliated with, ISO New England, Eversource, the U.S. Energy Information Administration, or any utility or government agency. Nothing here is operational, billing-quality, or investment-grade data.

## General

- All data comes from publicly available APIs and government datasets. Endpoints can change or disappear without notice.
- Fetched values are stored and displayed as received (after documented cleaning); no data values are invented, interpolated, or generated by AI.
- Timestamps are normalized to hourly resolution; rows that fail validation (missing timestamps, non-positive demand, unparseable values) are dropped and counted, not filled.

## Per-source caveats

| Source | Caveat |
|---|---|
| ISO-NE CSV downloads (demand, fuel mix, forecast, LMP) | Real-time values are **preliminary**, not settled data. Public export endpoints have no SLA and can change format. |
| EIA API v2 (hourly load, retail prices, natural gas prices) | EIA-930 hourly data contains imputed and later-corrected values; monthly price series lag by 2–3 months. |
| NOAA CDO weather | Station observations only; a single station is a proxy, not a regional composite. |
| NREL PVWatts | Modeled solar estimates, not measured generation. |
| AFDC EV stations | Station registry can lag real-world openings/closures. |
| OpenEI utility rates | Community-maintained database; rates may be outdated — verify against utility tariff sheets. |
| EPA eGRID | Annual average emission rates for the subregion — not hourly or marginal emissions. |
| EPA EJScreen / CDC SVI | Screening-level indicators with documented uncertainty; not suitable for site-level decisions. `-999` marks tracts with insufficient data (SVI). |
| FEMA NFHL flood zones | Query results may be capped (≈2,000 records); zones are regulatory boundaries, not flood predictions. |
| EIA Form 861 AMI | Annual self-reported utility filings. |
| Mock electricity demand | **Synthetic.** Exists for tests and offline development only; excluded from production source lists and never used in published charts. |

## Interpretation limits

- Temperature–demand relationships shown here are illustrative of a well-known driver; they are not forecasting models.
- Wholesale LMP is not the retail price households pay.
- Fuel mix reflects generation within ISO-NE and excludes the upstream mix of imported power.
